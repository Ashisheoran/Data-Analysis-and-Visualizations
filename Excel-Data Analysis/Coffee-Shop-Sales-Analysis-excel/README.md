# Coffee Shop Sales Analysis
## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze coffee shop retail sales data to derive actionable insights that help improve business performance, customer experience, and revenue.

## Dataset Overview

- The analysis is based on transactional sales data and covers:

- Sales revenue

- Customer footfall

- Product categories and sizes

- Store locations

- Time-based trends (day and hour)


## Key Business Questions Answered
### 1. Sales by Day and Hour

- Sales peak during morning hours (7 AM – 10 AM).

- The highest order volume occurs around 8–9 AM.

- Weekdays show more consistent and higher sales than weekends.

- Sales decline significantly after 7 PM.

### 2. Peak Sales Times

- Morning hours are the most profitable.

- Midday sales remain stable but lower than morning peaks.

### 3. Overall Sales Performance

- Total Sales: $698,812.33

- Total Footfall: 149,116 customers

- Average Bill per Person: $4.69

- Average Orders per Person: 1.44

### 4. Sales by Store Location

- Hell’s Kitchen records the highest sales.

- Astoria closely follows.

- Lower Manhattan has comparatively lower footfall and revenue.

### 5. Average Spend per Customer

- Customers typically purchase 1–2 items per visit.

- Low average bill value indicates strong potential for upselling.

### 6. Top Products by Revenue

- Barista Espresso

- Brewed Chai Tea

- Hot Chocolate

- Gourmet Brewed Coffee

- Brewed Black Tea

### 7. Sales by Product Category

- Coffee contributes approximately 39% of total sales.

- Tea contributes around 28%.

- Bakery contributes about 12%.

- Other categories have minimal impact.

###  8. Product Size Preference

- Regular and Large sizes dominate orders.

- Small size accounts for a minor share of total orders.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel

- Pivot Tables

- Charts and Dashboards

- Slicers for interactive analysis


## Key Insights

- Morning hours drive the majority of revenue.

- Coffee and tea are the core revenue-generating categories.

- Store location significantly impacts sales performance.

- Upselling and combo offers can increase average order value.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how sales data analysis can uncover customer behavior patterns and operational insights, enabling data-driven decision-making for retail businesses.
